# Logo Placement

Where the logo sits on each item, how big it is, and which variant goes there. These are the
positions printers, embroiderers and app designers use. A mockup that ignores them looks wrong
even when the logo itself is fine.

## Photo First, Then Position

A placement only looks right on a photo that shows the surface square-on.

- **Pick front-facing photos.** A shirt on a hanger, a cap facing the camera, a card lying flat.
  Avoid crumpled, rotated or worn-on-body shots for apparel: the logo can't follow folds, so it
  floats.
- **Measure before placing.** Find the item's reference points in photo pixels (centre front,
  collar point, buttons, seams, crown top, brim line, card corners), and work out the photo's
  scale from a known size (shirt body width ≈ 50 cm, button spacing ≈ 7 cm, CR80 card 54 × 86 mm).
- **Keep the logo level with the garment.** Rotate it only if the item itself is rotated in the
  photo, and then match the item's axis exactly.

## Apparel

| Item | Position | Size | Variant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tee, full front | Centred on the centre front; top of the print 7–10 cm below the collar | 25–30 cm wide (about 50–55% of chest width) | Colour on light fabric; reversed/knockout on dark |
| Tee or polo, left chest | Centre about 10 cm to the wearer's left of the centre front, level with the 2nd button (≈ 18–23 cm below the high point of the shoulder) | 8–10 cm wide | Lockup; embroidered, so simplified mark if under 25 mm tall |
| Upper back | Centred, top 5–8 cm below the collar | 20–25 cm wide | Wordmark or lockup |
| Sleeve | Centred on the sleeve, 5–8 cm above the hem | 5–7 cm | Mark |
| Cap, front | Centred on the front seam; vertical centre about 55% down from the crown top to the brim | 40% of crown width, ≤ 55 mm tall | Stacked lockup or mark, embroidered |
| Lanyard + ID card | Logo in a top band of the card (about a quarter of the card height); photo, name, role and ID below; accent strip at the foot | Card 54 × 86 mm portrait | Reversed lockup in the band |
| Lanyard strap | Repeat along the strap | Every 100–150 mm | Mono wordmark |

Left chest is small by design. In a full-garment view, add a magnified inset of the embroidery
instead of enlarging the logo.

## Print

| Item | Position | Variant |
|---|---|---|
| Name card front | Logo top-left; name, role and contacts bottom-left | Colour lockup |
| Name card back | Mark centred, about a third of the card height | Mark on brand colour, foil optional |
| Flyer / poster | Logo in the header band, top-left; QR bottom-right; contacts bottom-left | Reversed lockup in a dark header |
| Roll-up | Stacked lockup in the top quarter (the bottom third is hidden by tables and people) | Stacked |
| Banner | Lockup centred, tagline under it, kept inside the flat area away from grommets and sag | Lockup |

## Merchandise and Packaging

| Item | Position | Size | Variant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Box lid | Centred | About 35–40% of lid width | Stacked |
| Mug | On the face that's outward when held in the right hand (handle on the left as seen) | 7–9 cm wide | Colour lockup |
| Tote | Centred, slightly above the middle of the bag | 20–25 cm wide | Stacked, one spot colour |
| Notebook | Centred, or lower-right corner for a quieter look | 25–35 mm mark | Simplified mark, deboss |
| Bottle / tumbler | Centred on the front, in the upper half of the straight wall | 20–30 mm tall | Mono, engraved |
| Pen | Barrel, reading from clip to tip | 30–40 mm long | Mono lockup |

## Digital

| Item | Position | Variant |
|---|---|---|
| Web app | Top-left of the sidebar or top bar, with clear space all round | Reversed lockup on a dark sidebar, colour on light |
| Browser tab | Favicon | Micro mark |
| Splash screen | Centred horizontally, slightly above the vertical centre (optical centre); endorsement line near the bottom | Stacked reversed on the brand ground |
| Home screen | App icon among other icons at real size | Full mark at tile ÷ φ |
| Laptop / phone mockups | Content fills the screen exactly, clipped to the screen's rounded corners; redraw the notch or camera over the content | — |

## Review Pass

For every item, for every concept, check:

- [ ] The logo sits at the standard position for that item, measured from reference points
- [ ] Its size matches the real-world size range at the photo's scale
- [ ] It follows the item's axis (level on a level shirt; matched on a tilted sheet)
- [ ] The surface under it is flat and front-facing; if not, the photo is replaced
- [ ] No other brand marks remain on the item (labels, emblems, prints)
- [ ] Small placements (left chest, pen, sleeve) have a magnified inset
